# MVP Site Map

## Purpose

This document defines the approved five-page minimum viable structure for the SyNERDgy Solutions public consulting website. The MVP favors clarity, credibility, and launch speed over publishing every possible page before a visitor has even arrived.

## Primary navigation

1. Home
2. Services
3. Capabilities
4. About
5. Contact

## Page structure

### Home

**Purpose:** Establish the problem SyNERDgy solves, introduce the service model, show tangible outputs, explain the brand metaphor, and direct qualified visitors toward capabilities or contact.

**Planned sections:**

1. Hero
   - Headline: “Order isn’t accidental. It’s engineered.”
   - Category label: Enterprise Business Architecture
   - Supporting line: “We align strategy, governance, operations, and technology to turn organizational complexity into systems that work.”
   - Scope line: Governance · Operations · Process Design · Documentation · Technology & AI
   - Primary CTA: Explore Our Capabilities
   - Secondary CTA: Work With SyNERDgy
2. Problem recognition
   - Headline: “We find the loose threads.”
3. Services overview
   - Headline: “The problem usually lives between the parts. So do we.”
4. Public method
   - Headline: “We don’t patch systems. We rebuild them.”
   - Phases: Understand, Test, Design, Implement
5. Deliverables
   - Headline: “Practical structure. Visible accountability.”
6. Brand story
   - Headline: “Structure in every cell. Strength at every seam.”
7. Contact CTA
   - Headline: “Coming apart at the seams? That’s where we work.”

### Services

**Purpose:** Explain services through the operating problems they address, the work SyNERDgy performs, typical deliverables, and fit conditions.

**Working service categories:**

1. Governance, Risk and Compliance
2. Operations and Process Improvement
3. Evidence and Records Architecture
4. Research and Decision Support
5. AI Governance and Responsible Technology
6. Human-Centered Systems Design

The five-versus-six category discrepancy remains an open item until final service architecture review.

### Capabilities

**Headline:** “From fragmented process to defensible performance.”

**Purpose:** Present core capabilities, delivery approach, representative work products, confidentiality and responsible-AI practices, remote delivery scope, contracting readiness, and selected proof such as Governance Hive.

**MVP consolidation:**

- Verified contracting information belongs in a dedicated section on this page.
- Governance Hive and future public resources may be introduced here as representative work.
- Unverified registrations, identifiers, and downloads remain omitted.

### About

**Headline:** “Strong systems begin where the pieces meet.”

**Purpose:** Explain the company’s purpose, cross-functional operating philosophy, hive and seam metaphors, human-centered approach, and selected verified leadership roles.

The page is not a founder autobiography and must exclude private medical, family, and legal information.

### Contact

**Headline:** “Coming apart at the seams? That’s where we work.”

**Purpose:** Give buyers a direct, low-friction way to start a conversation and explain what information helps SyNERDgy understand an inquiry.

**MVP contact method:**

- Public email: `synerdgysolutions@gmail.com`
- No form, tracking script, or third-party intake service until separately approved.
