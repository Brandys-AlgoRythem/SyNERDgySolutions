# Public Content Sources and Controls

## Purpose

This file records the authority for public website content and the rules for translating internal source material into public-facing copy. It is not a dump of private source documents.

## Source hierarchy

When sources differ, use this order:

1. Final decisions in **SyNERDgy Website & Brand Project Hub**, especially the Decision Log
2. Approved language in **SyNERDgy Brand Messaging System — Seams & Hive**
3. Verified company facts in approved capability and registration materials
4. Newly drafted language that introduces no unsupported factual claim
5. Clearly documented internal open items when verification is incomplete

## Content classifications

Every major section should be classified during drafting:

- **Approved:** Reproduces or closely follows final approved wording
- **Adapted:** Condenses approved source material without changing meaning
- **Newly drafted:** Original public copy based on approved positioning
- **Requires verification:** Contains a factual field that must be checked before publication
- **Internal only:** Must not appear in the public repository or website

## Metadata and structured-data treatment

- Page titles and descriptions are adapted from approved public page copy.
- Organization structured data publishes only verified company identity, establishment date, public email, locality, service area, and approved capability categories.
- The favicon and social preview are controlled MVP brand assets, not claims that the final corporate logo is approved.
- Absolute canonical, Open Graph image, and sitemap URLs are generated only after the real production origin is approved.
- The 404 page is marked `noindex` and excluded from the sitemap.

## Public repository rules

The repository may contain:

- Public website code and assets
- Approved or adapted public marketing copy
- Public contact details intentionally selected for publication
- Sanitized representative work
- Public metadata, sitemap, and accessibility information
- Documentation describing publication controls without exposing private source contents

The repository must not contain:

- Passwords, API keys, recovery codes, tokens, or private certificates
- Portal credentials or private account identifiers
- Private Drive, Notion, email, or document-editing links
- Legal filings, claims evidence, medical records, family information, or private addresses
- Internal strategy documents or complete source exports
- Pricing, rates, or financial information not intentionally approved for publication
- Claims of clients, contracts, certifications, registrations, or results that have not been verified
- Private research notes or the complete internal methodology

## Claim-control rules

- Do not invent missing facts.
- Do not use `TBD` on public pages when omission is cleaner.
- Do not describe representative work as paid-client past performance.
- Do not claim government contract performance unless verified.
- Do not publish reconstructed experience hours as headline proof.
- Do not publish testimonials without permission and source verification.
- Do not imply legal advice, legal representation, certification authority, or guaranteed compliance outcomes.
- Keep AI-governance language practical and control-focused rather than promotional.

## Repository-source rule

Source documents remain in their controlled systems. The public repository records source names and content treatment, but does not copy private source files or expose edit links.
