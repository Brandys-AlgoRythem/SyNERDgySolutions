Recognize the operating problem

The warning signs are usually specific.

Instructions live with one employee. Records are spread across tools. Nobody owns the handoff. Reports arrive late, corrective actions stay open, and staff spend more time finding information than using it. SyNERDgy maps the breakdown and identifies what must be rebuilt.

01

Knowledge trapped with one person

Critical instructions, contacts, and decisions are held by one employee instead of being documented in a process the rest of the organization can use.

02

Requirements scattered across systems

Policies, contract terms, deadlines, and reporting rules sit in separate files or tools, so staff cannot see the complete set of obligations.

03

No clear owner at the handoff

Work moves between teams, vendors, or systems without a named owner, due date, escalation path, or clear definition of done.

04

Records that cannot explain the decision

Documents exist, but they do not show what happened, which requirement applied, who approved the action, or what evidence supports the result.

05

Corrective actions that never close

Findings are recorded, but ownership, evidence, deadlines, and closure checks are missing—so the same issue returns in the next review.

06

Tools that add administrative work

Staff re-enter the same information, maintain side spreadsheets, or work around software because the process and technology were never designed together.
