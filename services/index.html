Problem first

The formal service name matters less than the failure you need to stop.

Organizations rarely experience one tidy problem at a time. A missed requirement may also be a workflow failure, a records problem, an unclear handoff, and a technology issue.

SyNERDgy traces the connected causes, defines the boundary of the work, and builds practical outputs that can be owned after the engagement ends.

01

When requirements do not translate into daily work

Governance, Risk & Compliance

Connect authority, obligations, decision rights, controls, ownership, and proof so compliance becomes an operating system rather than a folder of policies.

What we help build

  • Governance frameworks and role structures
  • Regulatory and authority-source inventories
  • Policy-to-process and obligation crosswalks
  • Internal controls, risk ownership, and corrective paths
  • Monitoring, reporting, and closure structures

Typical outputs

  • Compliance matrices
  • Risk and control registers
  • Governance calendars
  • Responsibility matrices
  • Corrective-action plans

02

When work depends on memory, heroics, or informal handoffs

Operations & Process Improvement

Map how work actually moves, identify failure points and dependencies, and design repeatable workflows with clear ownership, exceptions, and completion criteria.

What we help build

  • Current-state and future-state workflow maps
  • Handoff, approval, escalation, and exception paths
  • Standard operating procedures and playbooks
  • Operating models, role maps, and continuity structures
  • Implementation and stabilization plans

Typical outputs

  • Process maps
  • SOP libraries
  • Decision trees
  • Implementation roadmaps
  • Continuity binders

03

When records exist but cannot explain the decisions

Evidence & Records Architecture

Organize records, sources, decisions, and evidence so information can be found, reconciled, traced, reviewed, and used to support action.

What we help build

  • Records inventories, file plans, and naming standards
  • Evidence, source, issue, and decision logs
  • Chronologies and conflicting-record reconciliation
  • Work-paper and submission-control systems
  • Paper-to-digital and repository-readiness plans

Typical outputs

  • Evidence inventories
  • Source-controlled chronologies
  • Folder architectures
  • Access registers
  • Audit-ready work papers

04

When the decision is complex and the evidence is scattered

Research & Decision Support

Bring primary sources, standards, stakeholder evidence, operational facts, and alternatives into a controlled analysis that supports a clear decision.

Research and regulatory analysis are informational and operational services. SyNERDgy does not provide legal representation or legal advice.

What we help build

  • Primary-source and comparative research
  • Policy, program, barrier, burden, and implementation analysis
  • Stakeholder research and qualitative evidence structures
  • Finding, limitation, and competing-explanation analysis
  • Traceable recommendations and monitoring measures

Typical outputs

  • Decision briefs
  • Executive summaries
  • Program analyses
  • Peer-practice reviews
  • Implementation-ready recommendations

05

When technology moves faster than accountability

AI Governance & Responsible Technology

Define when technology may be used, what information may enter it, where human judgment remains required, and how outputs are checked, documented, and retained.

What we help build

  • AI-use boundaries and disclosure rules
  • Human review and source-verification controls
  • Confidentiality, restricted-data, and retention controls
  • Bias, accuracy, scope, and license-risk review steps
  • Responsible automation and implementation requirements

Typical outputs

  • AI use policies
  • Review checklists
  • Risk registers
  • Human-approval workflows
  • Technology requirements

06

When the process technically works but people cannot reliably use it

Human-Centered Systems Design

Design operating systems around real cognitive load, communication needs, accessibility, time pressure, exceptions, and the conditions people face while doing the work.

What we help build

  • Accessible intake and communication workflows
  • Role-specific instructions and decision support
  • Low-friction forms, checklists, and interfaces
  • Exception paths for nonstandard operating conditions
  • Training and implementation materials for actual users

Typical outputs

  • User-centered workflows
  • Scripts and checklists
  • Accessible process guides
  • Training materials
  • Usability and readiness findings

Flexible scope, controlled work

Start with the smallest useful boundary.

An engagement may focus on one workflow, one compliance obligation, one records backlog, one decision, or a connected operating system. The scope should be narrow enough to control and broad enough to solve the real problem.

Diagnose

Find the failure points, dependencies, evidence gaps, and operating risks.

Build

Create the workflow, controls, records, tools, requirements, or operating structure.

Stabilize

Reduce backlog, restore visibility, assign ownership, and verify measurable closure.
