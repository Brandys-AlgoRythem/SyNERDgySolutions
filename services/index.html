Services

Practical support for work that is stuck, scattered, or overdue.

SyNERDgy helps buyers investigate a regulated program, diagnose a broken operation, rebuild a process, close corrective actions, stabilize an administrative backlog, or add a controlled workstream to a larger contract.

Problem first

Start with the failure that needs to stop.

A missed deadline may also involve unclear ownership, incomplete records, a broken handoff, and reporting that cannot show current status. Those are connected operating problems, not separate buzzwords.

SyNERDgy identifies the connected causes, agrees on a controlled scope, and produces work products the organization can keep using after the engagement ends.

01

When a buyer needs a defensible study, not a loose summary

Regulatory & Program Studies

Identify the governing sources, document how the program works in practice, gather relevant stakeholder and administrative evidence, and connect each supported finding to a practical recommendation.

Legal and regulatory work is provided as research, requirements mapping, policy support, and operational consulting—not as legal representation or individualized legal advice.

What we help build

  • Regulatory, policy, and program-source review
  • Process, role, and authority mapping
  • Stakeholder research and evidence protocols
  • Comparative and implementation analysis
  • Finding, recommendation, and monitoring design

Typical outputs

  • Authority crosswalks
  • Program and process maps
  • Evidence and limitation logs
  • Findings and recommendations
  • Decision-ready reports

02

When the cause of delay or inconsistency is not yet clear

Operational Diagnostics

Review how work moves today, where it stalls or duplicates, which records are missing, who owns each decision, and what creates backlog, continuity, or delivery risk.

What we help build

  • Current-state workflow and handoff review
  • Backlog, cycle-time, and dependency assessment
  • Role, ownership, and approval analysis
  • Documentation and continuity review
  • Root-cause and delivery-risk analysis

Typical outputs

  • Diagnostic findings
  • Workflow maps
  • Responsibility maps
  • Bottleneck assessments
  • Prioritized action plans

03

When the current process depends on workarounds or memory

Process Rebuilding

Turn informal or fragmented work into a repeatable process with clear steps, decisions, approvals, records, exceptions, ownership, and a practical implementation plan.

What we help build

  • Future-state workflows and task paths
  • Decision, approval, escalation, and exception rules
  • Standard operating procedures and playbooks
  • Forms, templates, checklists, and status controls
  • Training, rollout, and knowledge-transfer planning

Typical outputs

  • SOP libraries
  • Decision trees
  • Role and handoff maps
  • Implementation roadmaps
  • Continuity guides

04

When findings exist but remediation is late or hard to prove

Corrective-Action Support

Convert findings into assigned actions, evidence requirements, deadlines, status reporting, escalation rules, and closure checks that show whether the issue was actually corrected.

What we help build

  • Finding and root-cause review
  • Action ownership and due-date control
  • Evidence and verification requirements
  • Progress reporting and escalation paths
  • Closure and recurrence monitoring

Typical outputs

  • Corrective-action plans
  • Evidence trackers
  • Status dashboards
  • Closure checklists
  • Management reports

05

When routine work exceeds available administrative capacity

Administrative Surge Support

Restore control over intake, records, backlog, deadlines, reporting, and status when a launch, turnover, monitoring cycle, or workload spike overwhelms normal operations.

What we help build

  • Backlog inventory and controlled triage
  • Intake, request, and due-date tracking
  • Records cleanup and status reconstruction
  • Reporting overflow and document assembly
  • Continuity and knowledge-transfer support

Typical outputs

  • Triage plans
  • Backlog trackers
  • Cleaned records sets
  • Status reports
  • Continuity handoffs

06

When a prime or partner needs a defined, accountable workstream

Prime & Subcontract Support

Support a larger engagement with disciplined research, requirements traceability, technical writing, records control, project operations, quality review, and specialized systems analysis.

What we help build

  • Solicitation and requirement decomposition
  • Technical research and writing
  • Compliance and evidence matrices
  • Work-paper, document, and version control
  • Project tracking and deliverable assembly

Typical outputs

  • Compliance matrices
  • Technical narratives
  • Research memoranda
  • Work-paper systems
  • Submission-ready packages

Flexible scope, controlled work

Start with the smallest useful boundary.

An engagement may focus on one workflow, one compliance obligation, one records backlog, one decision, or one defined contract workstream. The scope should be narrow enough to control and broad enough to solve the real problem.

Focused assessment

Define the problem, current condition, evidence, risks, and practical next actions.

Project package

Build a defined workflow, control system, records structure, report, or implementation plan.

Temporary support

Reduce backlog, restore visibility, add controlled capacity, and transfer the work back with clear records.

Bring the operating problem

We will help define the right work.

Share what is failing, where it crosses people or systems, and what a stronger outcome must make possible.
