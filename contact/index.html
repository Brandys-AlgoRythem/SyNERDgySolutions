The first conversation establishes the working conditions.

Before substantive work begins, SyNERDgy confirms who may authorize decisions, what the scope requires, how information may be exchanged, which dates control, and what written authorization starts the work.

  1. 01

    Confirm the need

    Identify the operating problem, desired result, decision owner, known deadline, and people or systems involved.

  2. 02

    Confirm the engagement

    Verify authorized contacts, scope, contract status, insurance requirements when applicable, deliverables, and acceptance criteria.

  3. 03

    Confirm access and controls

    Agree on secure exchange methods, records access, reporting dates, communication expectations, and client-system requirements.

  4. 04

    Confirm commencement

    Document the schedule and notice to proceed. Work begins only after the required written authorization and commencement conditions are satisfied.

What helps us respond

A short, practical description is enough to start.

An initial inquiry does not need a polished statement of work. The following details help SyNERDgy understand the size, urgency, and operating boundary of the request.

What is happening now?

Describe the backlog, missed handoff, unclear requirement, overdue action, inconsistent report, documentation problem, or launch-readiness gap.

What needs to be different?

Name the practical result: a usable process, clean records, assigned corrective actions, a defensible study, temporary support, or a contract-ready workstream.

What controls the timing?

Include a required date, review cycle, solicitation deadline, monitoring event, turnover, launch, or other dependency that affects the schedule.

What information is restricted?

State whether the work may involve confidential, protected, client-owned, family, provider, employee, or regulated records—without attaching them to the first email.

Website terms of use

The website provides general information about SyNERDgy Solutions LLC and its capabilities. It is not a binding offer, contract, procurement response, legal opinion, financial audit, or individualized legal advice.

An engagement begins only through the applicable written agreement, authorized scope, required approvals, and notice to proceed. Website descriptions do not replace solicitation terms, contract requirements, or client-approved work plans.

Records, confidentiality & communication
