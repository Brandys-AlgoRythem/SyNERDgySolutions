Representative applied work

Governance Hive

Governance Hive is SyNERDgy-developed representative work that explores how a small organization can keep governance records, roles, obligations, licenses, continuity, dependencies, and organizational health visible in one local-first system.

It demonstrates capability in governance software planning, information architecture, role and permission design, compliance tracking, continuity signals, data structuring, and human-centered dashboard design.
